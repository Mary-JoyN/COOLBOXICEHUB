CUSTOM EVENT BRANDING

How Custom Bottled Water Branding Works

Available for 500 ml still bottled water (and 330 ml on enquiry). Minimum quantities, artwork charges and printing charges apply and are confirmed on your quotation.

  1. Select product & quantity (minimum order quantities apply for custom runs).
  2. Supply wording or artwork (PNG, JPG, WebP or PDF up to 5 MB; stored privately for your enquiry).
  3. Receive & approve your design proof before any printing begins.
  4. Complete payment requirement — the production lead-time clock starts only after final proof approval and required payment.
  5. Production & collection or delivery at Shop 14, Makalani Mall or scheduled route.
