Ordering & delivery

Book strictly 48–72 hours before fulfilment. Thursday delivery: 08:00–10:00 or 16:00–17:00, Windhoek time.

Free delivery within 25 km requires at least three different catalogue products, each meeting its minimum order. Thursday only: 08:00–10:00 or 16:00–17:00. The team verifies the address and distance. Regular and gourmet of one bag size count as one product.

Payments are non-refundable. Cancellation notice: at least six hours before collection or delivery. No VAT is added to displayed prices.
