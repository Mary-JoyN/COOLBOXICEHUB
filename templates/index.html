Explore all questions ↗
How can I message or contact CoolerBox?

You can email our team directly at info@coolerboxicehub.com or message us on WhatsApp at +264 85 248 5426. We respond promptly with quotes, availability, and delivery options.

Can you help me work out how much to order?

Yes. Use our built-in Guest Calculator above or in the ordering flow. Adjust your guest numbers and allowances, then review the estimated quantities.

Can you supply personalised bottled water for our event?

Yes. We supply 330ml and 500ml purified water with personalised labels for funerals, birthdays, weddings and corporate occasions. Artwork proofs are approved directly with our team.

What is the difference between regular and gourmet ice cubes?

Regular ice cubes are solid, versatile cubes for chilling coolers and bulk drink buckets. Gourmet ice cubes are rounded and hollow-centred, providing slow melting and an elegant presentation in glasses and cocktails.
