How this calculator works

Estimated drink ice = guests × kg per guest × (1 + reserve ÷ 100). Estimated water = guests × litres per guest × (1 + reserve ÷ 100). The default 0.5 kg ice, 1 litre water and 20% reserve are illustrative website settings.

Each estimated category is split equally among its selected automatic products, then rounded up to whole catalogue packs. Rounding may add a little extra. Manual quantities are additional to these allowances. Refill requests use litres and round up to listed containers. Cups are never converted into kilograms.

Prices come from the editable catalogue, including applicable bulk rates. Missing prices and personalised labels require a quotation. Delivery is excluded. Product images and marked prices remain samples.
