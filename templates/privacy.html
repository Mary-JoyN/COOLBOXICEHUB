YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy, in
plain language.

This notice describes the current CoolerBox website sample.

What we collect

Enquiry references, your sign-in account identifier, name, phone number, email, business name, selected products and quantities, requested dates, collection or delivery details, and enquiry notes. Event requests can include an event name, occasion, venue, on-site contact and uploaded artwork.

If you submit a review, we also store its star rating, title, comment, chosen display name, account identifier and any optional enquiry reference. Reviews are saved as drafts for moderation. Only the display name you choose (or Anonymous customer), rating, title and comment are published with your permission; the reference and account identifier are not displayed.

Why we use it

To prepare and manage your enquiry, discuss a formal quotation and artwork on WhatsApp, arrange supply, show your enquiry status and keep a record of agreed production confirmations. Do not add someone else’s contact details or photos unless you have permission to share them.

Who can access it

The signed-in customer can view their own enquiry status and notifications. The CoolerBox owner can manage enquiries and view uploaded artwork. Artwork downloads are restricted to the uploader and owner. Reviews or gallery content are published separately by the owner; enquiry artwork is not placed in the public gallery automatically.

WhatsApp and email

Opening a draft prepares a message in WhatsApp or your email app. You decide whether to send it. Formal quotations and artwork proofs are handled or discussed on WhatsApp. Information you send there is also processed by the service you use. The site does not automatically forward WhatsApp conversations to email.

Storage and retention

Enquiries, in-app updates and artwork are stored with the hosted website. The current sample has no automatic deletion period configured. Contact CoolerBox to request a correction or removal and quote your reference. A retention policy should be agreed before using the site for real customer orders.

Browser use

A temporary browser-session draft may carry product selections from the estimator to the enquiry form. Hosted sign-in is used to restrict access to customer tracking and owner controls. No advertising tracker has been added by this website implementation.

Questions or corrections

Use the current contact links below. Temporary contact details are used while this remains a sample.

Local supplier applications
